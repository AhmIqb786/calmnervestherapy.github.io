About your therapist

A calm, steady space to be heard

I'm Ahmed — I help people move from constant alertness to a place of steadiness, one honest conversation at a time.

Over the years, I've supported more than 200 clients across the world through stress, anxiety, burnout, and the kind of overwhelm that quietly builds up in a fast-moving life. My approach blends evidence-based methods with a warm, unhurried presence — whether we're meeting one-to-one or as part of a small group.

There's no pressure and no assumptions at the first meeting. We simply talk, and figure out together what steady would look like for you.

Children
